## Head-to-head (judge criteria in order: on-subject → originality → wallet voice → 3 AM as joke → economy)

Finalists: #3 dead-man's switch, #8 snooze, #1 solar conscience, #7 out-of-office.

- **#3 vs #8 (snooze):** both on-subject and device-voiced. Originality: a
  dead-man's switch mistaking you for a ghost is a more surprising mechanism
  than snooze, a familiar metaphor applied freshly. 3 AM: the ghost leans on
  the witching hour itself (3 AM = ghost hour); snooze leans on deferral to
  9 AM. Economy: snooze is tighter (109 vs 118), but every word in #3 earns
  its place. **Winner: #3**, on originality + hour-intrinsicness.
- **#3 vs #1 (solar conscience):** solar's "conscience charges by sunlight"
  is fresh, but the word "charges" sits next to the battery-charging cliché
  the judge brief explicitly warns against — a real risk with this judge.
  #3 has no cliché overlap at all. **Winner: #3.**
- **#3 vs #7 (out-of-office):** out-of-office is strong ("in its inbox,
  unread"), but office/auto-reply is a more familiar metaphor than a
  dead-man's switch; #3's kicker ("haunting your own portfolio") is the
  sharper surprise. **Winner: #3.**
